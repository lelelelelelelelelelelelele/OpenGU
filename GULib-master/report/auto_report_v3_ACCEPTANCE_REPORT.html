Append and noise policy

The stream appends real starts, first terminal transitions, retries, errors, and the first unchanged complete-cell skip. It omits dry-runs, internal cache probes, fixed next-step prose, duplicate runner/child terminals, and repeated unchanged HIT/skip observations. Dedup never rewrites an accepted line.
