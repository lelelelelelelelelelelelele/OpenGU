\paragraph{Interpretation boundaries.}
GraphEraser and GraphRevoker are partition-based ensembles. Their own pre-deletion ensemble endpoints differ from the canonical single-model GCN endpoint. Their after scores and matched GU--retraining gaps remain reportable, but the latter can include architecture and aggregation differences and should not be described as pure forgetting error.
