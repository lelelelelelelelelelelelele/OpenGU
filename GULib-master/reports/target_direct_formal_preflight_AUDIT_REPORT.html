Literature-only rationale for last-layer scope

This is a feasibility rationale, not evidence that last-layer and all-parameter influence are equivalent on these GCNs.

  • Koh & Liang (ICML 2017): scalable IF still depends on gradients and Hessian-vector products.
  • Pruthi et al. (NeurIPS 2020): TracIn explicitly uses layer cherry-picking as a scaling mechanism.
  • Yeh et al. (NeurIPS 2022): all-parameter tracing is often infeasible and last-layer selection is common, but last-layer influence can suffer cancellation in language models.
  • FastIF (EMNLP 2021): standard IF cost scales poorly and needs search/IHVP approximations.
  • Scaling Up Influence Functions: Arnoldi approximation is introduced to reach full-size Transformers.

The formal claims are therefore limited to the reviewed last_layer target-direct estimators. Any future all-parameter study requires a separate cost/approximation contract and fresh Cache V2 identity.
